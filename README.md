### 📡 [Radome Structural & Electromagnetic Optimization](https://github.com/sivaram-r-10/your-radome-project-repo)
> **Defence Research and Development Organisation Project** — A comparative analysis of 60-degree and 90-degree radome orientations to evaluate aerodynamic loads and radiation performance.

* 🛠️ **Tools Used:** ANSYS Workbench, HFSS, SolidWorks
* 🔬 **Materials Analyzed:** Carbon Fiber Reinforced Polymer (CFRP) & Quartz Fiber Composites
* 📈 **Key Achieved Metrics:** Evaluated structural deformation, peak stress distributions, and peak gain at 3.5 GHz to optimize structural integrity under extreme wind velocities.
